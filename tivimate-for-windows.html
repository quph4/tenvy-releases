Looking for TiviMate on Windows?

TiviMate is one of the best IPTV players for Android TV, but there is no Windows version. Tenvy is a free player built for the PC that works the way TiviMate fans expect: a real TV guide, channel numbers and fast zapping.

Updated 9 November 2026

Why not just run TiviMate in an emulator?

You can try running TiviMate in an Android emulator like BlueStacks, but it was made for TV remotes, not a mouse and keyboard, and many people find it slow or unreliable that way. A native Windows app is simply nicer to use on a PC.

What Tenvy has that TiviMate users look for

  • A full TV guide — every channel on a timeline, with search, reminders and day-by-day browsing.
  • Catch-up — watch programmes that already aired, straight from the guide, on channels your provider archives.
  • Channel numbers and zapping — type a number, or use the arrow keys, just like a TV.
  • Favorites in your own order, and hidden channels and categories you never watch.
  • Films and series with posters, resume and auto-play of the next episode.
  • Mini player that stays on top while you work.
  • Parental PIN, including locks by keyword.

What it doesn't do (yet)

Tenvy doesn't record yet, and it only runs on Windows. If recording matters to you, tell us on Discord — what users ask for is what gets built next.

Use the same login as in TiviMate

Tenvy works with the same details you use in TiviMate: an Xtream Codes login (server, username and password) or an M3U playlist link. Paste it on Tenvy's first screen and your channels, guide, films and series load by themselves.

Questions

Is Tenvy free?

Yes. No ads, no account and nothing to pay. If you like it you can support it with a tip, but it's entirely optional.

Does Tenvy include channels?

No. Like TiviMate, Tenvy is a player only. You need your own IPTV subscription, and should only use services you have the right to watch.

Can I move my TiviMate favorites over?

Not automatically — TiviMate keeps them on your Android device. Adding favorites in Tenvy takes a right-click per channel.
