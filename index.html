FAQ

Questions

Does Tenvy include any channels?

No. Tenvy is a player only — it doesn't include, sell or link to any channels, films or series. You sign in with the details from your own IPTV provider.

What do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC and an IPTV subscription that gives you an Xtream Codes login (server, username, password) or an M3U playlist link.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads and no account. If you like it, you're welcome to support development — entirely optional.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that safe?

That message appears for new apps that haven't yet built up a reputation with Microsoft. Click More info → Run anyway. Only download Tenvy from this page.

How do updates work?

Tenvy checks for new versions on its own and installs them with one click. Every update is cryptographically signed.

What about my privacy?

Your provider login stays on your PC. Tenvy talks to your provider to play and list content, and to this site to check for updates and settings. There are no accounts, analytics or ads. Read the privacy policy.

Does catch-up work on every channel?

*Catch-up depends on your provider. Channels your provider archives get a catch-up marker in the guide.
