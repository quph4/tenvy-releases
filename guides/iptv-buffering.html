IPTV buffering or freezing on your PC?

Buffering has many causes, and the fix depends on which one it is. Test one thing at a time and you'll usually find it in a few minutes.

Updated 10 November 2026

1. Is it one channel or all of them?

If only one channel buffers, the problem is that stream at the provider. Try another channel; if that's fine, report the channel to your provider.

2. Is it only at busy times?

Buffering during big football matches or in the evening usually means your provider's servers are overloaded. There's nothing to fix on your side — it passes when the peak does.

3. Too many connections at once

Most subscriptions allow one or two streams at a time. If the app is also open on your TV or phone, streams get cut off or refuse to start. Close it elsewhere.

4. Wi-Fi

Live TV needs a steady connection, especially in HD and 4K. If you can, try a network cable for a minute — if the buffering stops, it's your Wi-Fi.

5. Your internet provider

Some internet providers slow down streaming traffic. If everything else checks out and buffering happens on every channel, at every time of day, a VPN can tell you: if it's smooth through the VPN, your internet provider is the cause.

Questions

Will a different player fix buffering?

Only if the player was the problem — for example one that gives up instead of reconnecting. Most buffering comes from the stream, the provider or the network.
