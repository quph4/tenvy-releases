IPTV guide (EPG) not loading? Here's why

The guide grid appears, but the programme info never does. It's one of the most common IPTV problems on Windows, and it almost always comes down to one of these five things.

Updated 10 November 2026

1. The guide file is huge

A provider's XMLTV guide is often 50–200 MB, usually gzipped. Players that download it in the foreground, or give up after 30 seconds, never finish. Give it a few minutes the first time, and prefer a player that loads the guide in the background.

2. Channels don't match the guide

The guide is linked to channels by their tvg-id. Many playlists leave it empty, or spell it differently than the guide (SVT1.se vs svt1.se). A good player also matches on the channel's name — otherwise those channels stay blank.

3. The playlist doesn't say where the guide is

An M3U playlist names its guide in the first line: #EXTM3U url-tvg="…". If that's missing, the player has nothing to load. Ask your provider for the XMLTV (EPG) link and add it separately.

4. You're using an M3U link instead of the Xtream login

If your provider gave you a server, username and password (Xtream Codes), use those. The guide then comes from the panel itself and works much more reliably than through an M3U link.

5. The guide is an hour off

Some guides are published in the wrong time zone. If programmes consistently start an hour early or late, it's the data, not your clock. Look for a "guide time offset" setting.

Questions

How long should the guide take to load?

Usually under a minute or two, depending on its size and your connection. After that it updates in the background.

Some channels have a guide and some don't. Why?

Those channels aren't in the guide file, or their names and ids don't match it. Your provider can fix the playlist; there's little a player can do about missing data.
