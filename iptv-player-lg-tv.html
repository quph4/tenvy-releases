Tenvy for LG TV

Tenvy IPTV Player brings the IPTV service you already have to your LG TV: live TV with a guide and catch-up, films and series, in one quick app made for the remote. It's coming soon to the LG Content Store.

Updated 10 November 2026

What you get

  • Live TV that starts in about a second, with what's on now and next.
  • A programme guide, with catch-up on the channels your provider archives.
  • Films and series as covers, with the whole plot, seasons and episodes.
  • Resume where you stopped; the next episode starts by itself.
  • Favorites, history and search across channels, films and series.
  • Audio tracks and subtitles, with three subtitle sizes.
  • The arrow keys, or the Magic Remote's pointer and wheel.

Which TVs

LG TVs from 2021 on, with webOS 6 or later. 4K models come first; Full HD models follow with the next update.

What it costs

Tenvy is free for 14 days from the first start. After that it costs €10 once for your TV, with no subscription. You pay from your phone: the TV shows a code and a QR code, you open it, pay with PayPal or a card, and the TV switches on by itself within seconds. Gift codes work on the same page.

What you need

Your own IPTV subscription with an Xtream Codes login: a server address, a username and a password. Tenvy is a player only and includes no channels, films or series.

Help

Ask in #tv-help on Discord, or write to tenvyiptv@outlook.com.

Questions

When can I get it?

Tenvy is in LG's review now. As soon as it's in the LG Content Store, this page links to it and it's announced on Discord.

Is it a subscription?

No. After the 14 free days it's €10 once for each TV, and that's all.

Can I use it on more than one TV?

Yes. Each TV gets its own 14 free days and is activated on its own.

Does Tenvy include channels?

No. Tenvy is a player only. You need your own IPTV subscription, and should only use services you have the right to watch.

Is my login safe?

Your login stays on your TV and goes only to your own provider. Tenvy's licence server knows your TV only by a code. Read the privacy policy.

Is there a Windows version?

Yes, and it's free: Tenvy for Windows.
